Electoral Architecture

The 5 Election Tiers in Nigeria

Detailed guide to qualifications, voter distribution, and legal cycles for all Nigerian democratic elections.

Tier 1 • Executive Head of State

Presidential Election

4-Year Tenure • Nationwide Electorate

To be declared elected, a presidential candidate must secure the highest number of votes cast nationwide AND at least 25% of the total votes in at least two-thirds (24) of the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory.

View Presidential Candidates Simulate Presidential Ballot
Tier 2 • Subnational Executive

Governorship Election

36 States (28 Standard Cycle + 8 Off-Cycle)

Governors serve as chief executives of their respective states. Under Section 180(2) of the 1999 Constitution, a governor's tenure starts on the day they take the oath of office. Because Supreme Court rulings restored several stolen mandates at different times, 8 Nigerian states now hold off-cycle elections:

Off-Cycle Gubernatorial States (8 States):
• Anambra: November 2025
• Edo: September 2024
• Ondo: November 2024
• Ekiti: June 2026
• Osun: July 2026
• Kogi: November 2027
• Imo: November 2027
• Bayelsa: November 2027
Note: The Federal Capital Territory (FCT) has no governor, being administered directly by the Federal Minister.

Tier 3 • National Assembly (Upper Chamber)

Senatorial Election

109 Seats • 3 per State + 1 FCT

The Senate comprises 109 distinguished senators representing equal geographic zones across the country. Led by the President of the Senate, it exercises legislative powers and provides constitutional oversight.

About CivicVote & Voter Education

Demystifying BVAS, IReV, collation hierarchies, and Nigerian electoral jurisprudence.

📲

BVAS Authentication

Under Section 47(2) of Electoral Act 2022, BVAS biometrically validates voters via facial or fingerprint scans.

🌐

IReV Public Portal

Transmits photographed polling unit Form EC8A sheets directly to the cloud for real-time citizen observation.

📊

Collation Chain

Hierarchical audit trail: Polling Unit (EC8A) → Ward (EC8B) → LGA (EC8C) → State (EC8D) → National (EC8E).
