Constitutional Architecture

Nigeria's 5 Democratic Electoral Categories

The 1999 Constitution (as amended) and Electoral Act 2022 establish 5 tiers of democratic representation across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory.

Tier 1 • Head of State & Commander-in-Chief

Presidential Election

The highest office in Nigeria constitutes a single nationwide constituency encompassing all 36 States and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT). Under Section 134 of the 1999 Constitution, to be declared elected, a candidate must secure the highest number of votes cast nationwide AND at least 25% of the votes cast in at least two-thirds (24) of the 36 States and the FCT.

Tier 2 • Subnational Chief Executives

Governorship Elections (Standard & 8 Off-Cycle States)

State Governors serve as Chief Executives across Nigeria's 36 federating states. Importantly, not all states hold elections simultaneously. While 28 states follow the standard 4-year cycle, 8 states operate on Off-Cycle (Staggered) calendars due to court rulings restoring stolen mandates or nullifying elections. Under Section 299, the Federal Capital Territory has NO governor; the President exercises authority delegated to the Minister of the FCT.
